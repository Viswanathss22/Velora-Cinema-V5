VELORA CINEMA — COLLECTIONS V3
Front End Only / Noncommercial Student Prototype

Credits and sources of authentic film and television artwork

1. Film posters and cinematic backdrops
   The Movie Database (TMDb): https://www.themoviedb.org/
   Image CDN: https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/
   Some preselected movie records have corresponding known movie poster paths.

2. Additional movie posters (title and year matched)
   Apple iTunes Store Search API:
   https://performance-partners.apple.com/search-api
   Only images from catalogued films with an exact title and release year match
   are accepted. Apple and iTunes logos are not used.

3. Television show posters (title and year matched)
   TVmaze public API: https://www.tvmaze.com/api
   Search endpoint: https://api.tvmaze.com/search/shows?q=...
   TVmaze data attribution: TVmaze (CC BY-SA 4.0 as applicable to data).
   Poster copyright remains with the original owners.

4. Anime series artwork (canonical/alternate name and year matched)
   Jikan REST API: https://jikan.moe/
   Jikan is an unofficial MyAnimeList API, not endorsed by MyAnimeList.
   Image copyright remains with the original owners.

The app caches images under assets/posters and assets/backdrops after downloading.
No film/series/anime artwork is synthesized, and missing posters are visibly marked.
No images, movie clips, episodes or full videos are hosted by this project.
Do not redistribute downloaded posters as your own artwork.
